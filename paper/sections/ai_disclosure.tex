\section*{AI-Generated Content Disclosure}

Large language models were used as research and writing assistants
in the preparation of this work, under the author's direction. Their
roles were: drafting and copy-editing manuscript text; developing
and debugging experimental and analysis code; assisting the
audit pass, including locating internal inconsistencies and checking
references against publisher records; and drafting analysis reports
from computed outputs. Language models are additionally the
\emph{subject} of the benchmark in \S6.11, which is a methodological
use and is described there.

No result in this paper was generated by a language model. Every
reported number was produced by executing the released code on the
stated data, and the audit and validation passes recomputed the
headline quantities from the raw outputs and raw datasets. No AI
system is an author of this paper. The human author directed the
work, reviewed and edited all AI-assisted text and code, and takes
full responsibility for the paper's content, claims, and any
remaining errors.
